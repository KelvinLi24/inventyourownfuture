Fintech & Investment Banking
July 27–31, 2026 | 9 a.m. – 5 p.m. | Hong Kong PolyU | Free of charge

A five-day intensive bootcamp that takes upper secondary students inside the world of finance. Led by industry experts and researchers — including a veteran derivatives trader and an assistant professor from Hong Kong PolyU — students learn to analyse live market data, build real financial models, and pitch their own investment strategies.

The programme moves from the ground up: financial foundations and risk assessment in the early days, into modelling, valuations, derivatives, and hedging, before culminating in a capstone showcase where students present their work and compete for awards. Along the way, they build practical, career-relevant skills in Excel and financial modelling, portfolio construction, valuation and deal analysis, AI-powered analysis, and investor pitching.

Guided by Dr. Allen Tai Hoi-Lun of PolyU's Department of Applied Mathematics and Wilson Lee, Co-Head of Asia Equity Derivatives at SG Securities, this is a rare chance for students to learn how markets really work — and to imagine a future building within them. Open to upper secondary students and free of charge.

Swift App Development
July 6–10, 2026 | 9 a.m. – 5 p.m. | Hong Kong PolyU | Free of charge

A five-day intensive bootcamp where younger students learn to build real iOS apps from scratch. Led by industry experts and educators — including a veteran system architect and instructor from Hong Kong PolyU — students learn to code in Swift, design real applications, and pitch their own creations.

The programme builds step by step: from Swift foundations and programming basics, through logic, modules, and app refinement, to a final capstone showcase where students demo their apps and compete for awards. Throughout, they develop genuine technical and creative skills — Swift and SwiftUI programming, iOS app prototyping, UI/UX design thinking, data and state management, testing and debugging, and app pitch presentation.

Guided by Leo Lui Shing Fai, an instructor at PolyU and AI & EdTech system architect with over 20 years in technology education, this bootcamp gives students the tools to turn their ideas into working apps. Open to upper primary and lower secondary students and free of charge.

AI Innovation in Healthcare & Neuroscience
27 June 2026 | 4:00 PM – 5:00 PM | PolyU, Z211

At the intersection of AI, neuroscience, and healthcare innovation, industry leaders come together to explore how technology is reshaping medicine — and what it means for the next generation of healthcare professionals.

This Invent Your Own Future event brings students into direct conversation with some of Hong Kong's most accomplished figures in medicine and healthcare enterprise. Moderated by Preston Chan, Founder of IYOF, and Jacky Kwan, Chairman of the Primary Care Education Foundation, the discussion features an expert panel spanning clinical practice, pharmaceutical leadership, and health-tech entrepreneurship.

Panellists include Dr. Chan Chi Kwok, a urology specialist with over 30 years of experience; Mr. Tony Cheng, Managing Director of Merck; Dr. Eric Chu, Founder of NYMG; and Ms. Bikie Wong, Founder of Grand Brilliance Group Holdings. Together they offer students a rare, candid look at where AI and medicine are heading, and how to build a meaningful career at that frontier.

Whether you're considering a future in medicine, science, or technology, this session is an invitation to imagine what you could build.

Moderators

  • Preston Chan: Founder of IYOF; Political Science student at the University of Southern California (Class of 2028), Co-Founder of GreekLife, and published author of a policy brief on the global food price crisis.

  • Jacky Kwan: Chairman of the Primary Care Education Foundation and The Zebra Wellness Group; Co-Founder of Bamboos Healthcare (2293.HK), with economics degrees from HKU, CUHK, and Harvard.

Expert Panel

  • Dr. Chan Chi Kwok: Urology Specialist with over 30 years of expertise; holds dual fellowships from the Royal College of Surgeons of Edinburgh and the Hong Kong Academy of Medicine.

  • Mr. Tony Cheng: Managing Director of Merck; seasoned healthcare executive with over 20 years of senior leadership across multinational pharmaceutical organisations, with deep expertise in AI-driven clinical healthcare.

  • Dr. Eric Chu: Founder of NYMG and co-owner of EC Healthcare (2138.HK); prolific author of 100+ first-authored case reports on spinal disorders, and former Chairman of the Chiropractic Doctors Association of Hong Kong.

  • Ms. Bikie Wong: Founder of Grand Brilliance Group Holdings Ltd.; founder of a leading Greater Bay Area health-tech company and the Bright Side Foundation, championing COVID-19 community efforts and youth development through programs like 'Happy Kids.'
